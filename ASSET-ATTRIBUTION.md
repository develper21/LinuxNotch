`annoyed`, `approval`, `approve`, `attach`, `blip`, `close`, `dizzy`, `error`,
`finish`, `greeting`, `greet`, `gulp`, `hover`, `love`, `open`, `peek`, `pop`,
`proud`, `question`, `rate`, `search`, `send`, `slap`, `sleep`, `think`, `tick`,
